# CoreCamera

### Press the shutter. Wait. The photograph develops.

**[Download](https://github.com/stanochillart/CoreCamera/releases/download/v1.5/CoreCamera-1.5.apk)** · Android 8.0+ · no account · no ads

---

The phone corrects the photograph before you have seen it. Sharpens. Smooths. The shot is perfect. And nobody’s.

CoreCamera does not correct the world. It develops it.

Shutter. A short sound. First, white. Then a room, a face, grain, a thick white frame. Like a Polaroid you hid inside the phone. Sometimes light crosses the image that was never meant to be there. You will not take that shot again.

---

Two looks. Warm, old film. Or cold, night.

Two formats. Square. Or classic Polaroid with a thick frame.

You set the brightness. Front camera or back, wide or close. The gallery is only yours: nothing the phone shot while it was trying to be perfect.

The photograph stays on the device until you send it.

---

For anyone tired of every phone picture looking the same.

This is not a studio. This is not an app that improves your shot. If you want a clinically sharp photograph, this is not the instrument.
