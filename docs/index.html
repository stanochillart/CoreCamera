CoreCamera

Press the shutter. Wait. The photograph develops.

Download · Android 8.0+ · no account · no ads

The phone corrects the photograph before you have seen it. Sharpens. Smooths. The shot is perfect. And nobody's.

CoreCamera does not correct the world. It develops it.

Shutter. A short sound. First, white. Then a room, a face, grain, a thick white frame. Like a Polaroid you hid inside the phone. Sometimes light crosses the image that was never meant to be there. You will not take that shot again.

Two looks. Warm, old film. Or cold, night. Two formats. Square. Or classic Polaroid with a thick frame.

You set the brightness. Front camera or back, wide or close. The gallery is only yours. The photograph stays on the device until you send it.

For anyone tired of every phone picture looking the same. This is not a studio. If you want a clinically sharp photograph, this is not the instrument.
